Who We Are

Two Decades of Operational Excellence

Founded in 2005, Grover Infomedia Pvt. Ltd. (GIPL) has grown into a leading business solutions provider, empowering enterprises to thrive in a dynamic and ever-evolving global market. Initiated in 2008, our specialized BPO & Contact Center division combines seasoned domain professionals, custom-architected telephony and CRM software, and robust IT infrastructure.

Specializing in Verification, Sales, Marketing, Backend Support, Device Procurement, Data Entry & Validation, we empower marquee clients—including Gilat Satellite Networks, Everstylish, OnePlus, realme, Godfrey Phillips India, Call2Connect, and Aeroflot Airlines—to achieve increased sales, higher profitability, and amplified brand awareness.
